## Design Principles Applied

1. **Consistency**: All elements use the same glass morphism style
2. **Depth**: Multiple layers of blur and transparency create depth
3. **Interactivity**: Hover and tap animations enhance user experience
4. **Accessibility**: Sufficient contrast while maintaining glass aesthetic
5. **Performance**: Optimized blur and shadow effects
6. **Responsiveness**: Glass design works on all screen sizes

## Color Palette

- **Background**: Deep blue-black (`#0a0e1a`, `#0d1117`)
- **Glass Overlays**: White with varying opacity (5%-25%)
- **Accents**: Blue (`blue-500`), Cyan (`cyan-500`), Indigo (`indigo-500`)
- **Text**: White with 70-100% opacity
- **Borders**: White with 10-25% opacity

## Animation Details

- **Gradient Orbs**: 15-25 second cycles with easeInOut
- **Button Hover**: Scale 1.02-1.05 with smooth transitions
- **Button Tap**: Scale 0.95 for tactile feedback
- **Message Entry**: Fade in with slide animation
- **Typing Effect**: 30ms per character for first message
